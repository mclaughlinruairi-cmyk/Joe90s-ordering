About Us

Joe 90's is a family-run chip shop on Main Street, Irvinestown, serving traditional fish & chips, burgers, fried chicken and our own Sham Wrap and Munchie Box since day one. Everything is cooked fresh to order — nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting for you.

We're a small local business, so if you're passing through Fermanagh or you're a regular on Main Street, expect the same thing every time: hot, fresh, generous portions, and a friendly face behind the counter.
